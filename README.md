## About the business

SSC Group's public and archived business information connects the name to **Shree Shivalingeshwar Computers** in Haveri, Karnataka. Its services span computer training, desktop and laptop retail, technical support and security/technology solutions.

A historical KEONICS training-centre list names Shree Shivalingeshwara Computers in Haveri and an SSC Solutions centre in Hangal, supporting the group's longstanding local education identity. That record should **not** be interpreted as current approval of every individual course, programme or credential.

The SSC Group corporate website project presents four primary verticals:

- **SSC Computer Education:** practical computer training, course information and education enquiries.
- **Computer and laptop sales:** desktops, laptops, printers and relevant business and personal computing products.
- **SSC Repair Desk:** laptop and desktop servicing, maintenance, upgrades and technical support.
- **SSC Gadgets & Accessories:** peripherals, accessories and useful consumer technology.

CCTV and security solutions also appear in the broader SSC Haveri business identity and navigation materials. The group brand aims to help local customers access these related services through one recognisable business network.

Founding years, student totals, testimonials, founder details, certificate claims and other numeric statements visible in project artwork should be treated as **design-stage content unless corroborated by current business records**.

## The problem

A group offering computer courses, laptop purchases, repairs and consumer technology needs to communicate with very different visitors. A student may want a course schedule or counselling; a buyer needs an appropriate laptop or accessory; someone with a damaged computer wants a repair contact; and a business customer may need product supply or technical support.

A site focused only on one division would underrepresent the others. Combining everything into a single undifferentiated list would create the opposite problem: too many unrelated choices without a useful pathway.

The corporate website therefore needs **one recognisable group identity with separate service journeys**. It also needs to explain the group's local presence, introduce its businesses and make contacting the correct team uncomplicated, especially on mobile.

This describes the business and communication challenge supported by Tech Conversion's project record. It is not an independently measured usability problem.

## How Tech Conversion presented the group website

The points below describe the project presentation and saved website components, without suggesting that every draft component was published unchanged or tested in production.

1. **Establish one parent identity.** The homepage introduces SSC Group first and frames education, computer retail, repair and gadgets as related businesses beneath it.
2. **Make the business divisions recognisable.** A clear multi-card structure separates computer education, laptop/computer sales, repair services and gadgets/accessories.
3. **Separate the corporate story from specialist journeys.** About, Founder & Legacy, Our Businesses, Outlets, Gallery and Contact are group-level themes, while product shopping and course discovery belong to specialist routes.
4. **Show how the group serves Haveri.** The presentation uses education, retail and service contexts to communicate the breadth of the local business.
5. **Create a group-business network section.** Saved components identify SSC Computer Education, Shree Shivalingeshwar Computers, SSC Repair Desk and SSC Gadgets individually.
6. **Give the business a consistent visual system.** Deep navy and white, blue/orange accents, readable headings, clean cards and consistent enquiry actions create a unified group identity.
7. **Consider mobile navigation separately.** Saved mobile-navigation components include group pages, specialist service links and Call/WhatsApp actions. These demonstrate implementation work but do not independently prove live accessibility or functionality.
8. **Place enquiries near relevant information.** Contact actions appear alongside service discovery, reducing the distance between identifying a need and contacting the appropriate team.
9. **Keep business claims evidence-based.** Student counts, service promises, certification labels, founder details, warranties and dealer claims should be confirmed before being presented as verified facts.

## Standards and checks appropriate to the project

These are **review criteria**, not proof of completed accreditation or technical certification.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** evaluate image weight, loading, layout stability and responsiveness on group and service pages.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** review keyboard navigation, mobile-drawer focus management, labelled controls, contrast and form accessibility.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** check useful service descriptions, clear page titles, internal links and accurate local-business information.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** assess accurate Organisation, LocalBusiness, Service and BreadcrumbList markup where supported by approved content.
- **Course-recognition accuracy:** verify the issuing organisation, approved centre, certification scope, programme eligibility and current validity before using government-recognised or KEONICS-related claims.
- **Product and repair accuracy:** confirm brand/dealer authorisations, genuine-parts claims, warranty terms, repair capabilities and service coverage with current business records.
- **Local business consistency:** reconcile exact Haveri/Hangal branch names, addresses, telephone numbers and opening hours across the corporate website and local listings.
- **Enquiry privacy:** review the handling of student, customer and device-repair enquiries under applicable data-protection requirements.
