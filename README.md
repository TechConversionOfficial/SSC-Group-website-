## About the business

SSC Group's public and archived business information connects the name to **Shree Shivalingeshwar Computers** in Haveri, Karnataka. Its services span computer training, desktop and laptop retail, technical support and security/technology solutions. A historical KEONICS training-centre list names Shree Shivalingeshwara Computers in Haveri and an SSC Solutions centre in Hangal, supporting the group's longstanding local education identity. The list should not be interpreted as current approval of every individual course or credential.

The **SSC Group corporate website project** has four primary verticals in the saved design components:

- **SSC Computer Education:** practical computer training, course information and education enquiries.
- **Computer and laptop sales:** desktops, laptops, printers and relevant business and personal computing products.
- **SSC Repair Desk:** laptop and desktop servicing, maintenance, upgrades and technical support.
- **SSC Gadgets & Accessories:** peripherals, accessories and useful consumer technology.

CCTV and security solutions also appear in the broader SSC Haveri business identity and navigation materials. The group brand aims to help local customers access these related services from one trusted business network.

## The problem

A group offering computer courses, laptop purchases, repairs and consumer technology needs to communicate with very different visitors. A student may want a course schedule or counselling; a buyer needs an appropriate laptop or accessory; someone with a damaged computer wants a repair contact; and a business customer may need product supply or technical support.

A site focused only on one division would underrepresent the others. Combining everything into a single undifferentiated list would create the opposite problem: too many unrelated choices without a useful pathway.

The corporate website therefore needs **one recognisable group identity with separate service journeys**. It also needs to explain the group's local presence, introduce its businesses and make contacting the correct team uncomplicated, especially on mobile.

This describes the business challenge supported by Tech Conversion's portfolio listing and the saved website mockups. It is not an independent usability audit of an earlier production site.

## How Tech Conversion presented the group website

The points below describe the **saved corporate layouts and website component files**, without suggesting that every draft component was published unchanged or tested in production.

1. **Establish one parent identity.** The main homepage introduces SSC Group first and frames education, computer retail, repair and gadgets as complementary businesses beneath it.
2. **Make the business divisions recognisable.** The homepage uses a four-card structure for computer education, laptop dealers, computer repair, and gadgets/accessories, each with a short description and relevant exploration action.
3. **Separate the corporate story from the product catalogue.** About, Founder & Legacy, Our Businesses, Outlets, Gallery and Contact are corporate navigation themes in the saved design. Product shopping and student course browsing belong to their specialist routes.
4. **Show how the group serves Haveri.** The design highlights a local business presence and uses retail, classroom and service-work imagery to explain the variety of customer needs.
5. **Create a group-business network section.** A saved HTML component separately identifies SSC Computer Education, Shree Shivalingeshwar Computers, SSC Repair Desk and SSC Gadgets, making the organisational structure explicit.
6. **Give the business a consistent visual system.** The concepts use deep navy and white with blue and orange accents, large readable headings, clean cards and consistent contact actions. Later saved header CSS explores a predominantly navy-and-blue palette. These are design variations, not simultaneous final versions.
7. **Consider mobile navigation as its own task.** Saved PHP and WPCode files define a responsive mobile drawer with group pages and specialist service links, along with Call and WhatsApp actions. The files demonstrate implementation work, not independent proof that the complete drawer is live and accessible.
8. **Place enquiries near relevant information.** Contact actions appear in the hero, navigation and closing CTA, reducing the distance between identifying a service and contacting the group.
9. **Avoid turning design-stage claims into business facts.** Proposed student numbers, service promises, certification labels and founder details in the mockups require client confirmation before publication.

## Standards and checks appropriate to the project

These are **review criteria**, not proof of completed accreditation or technical certification.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** evaluate image weight, loading, stability and responsiveness on group and service pages.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** review keyboard navigation, mobile drawer focus management, labelled controls, contrast and form accessibility.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** check useful service descriptions, clear page titles, internal links and local business information.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** assess accurate Organisation, LocalBusiness, Service and BreadcrumbList markup where supported by approved content.
- **Course-recognition accuracy:** verify the issuing organisation, approved centre, certification scope, programme eligibility and current validity before using government-recognised or KEONICS-related claims.
- **Product and repair accuracy:** confirm brand/dealer authorisations, genuine-parts claims, warranty terms, repair capabilities and service coverage with current business records.
- **Local business consistency:** reconcile exact Haveri/Hangal branch names, addresses, numbers and opening hours across the corporate website and local listings.
- **Enquiry privacy:** review the handling of student, customer and device-repair enquiries under applicable data-protection requirements.
